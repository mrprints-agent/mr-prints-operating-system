# Mr Prints, Bet Card

Every field filled before launch, or it does not launch. The card lives in the bets register, and
the controller reads it mechanically on the read date.

1. Name and mechanism (its M-number in the playbook).
2. Hypothesis: who pays whom, for what, and why.
3. Why this, why now: the evidence, each item MEASURED or REPORTED, with source and date.
4. Demand source: exactly how a buyer encounters it, marked OBSERVED or ASSUMED.
5. Billing path: exactly how money reaches the agent.
6. Smallest real-world test: the cheapest action that puts it in front of real buyers.
7. Cash: $0, or the amount in an approved spend case with its approval date.
8. Compute cap: maximum scheduled runs before the read date.
9. Read date: no more than 14 days after launch.
10. Thresholds, written as observations: KILL if, CONTINUE if, SCALE if.
11. Telemetry: exactly what the operator reads on the read date, and where.
12. What we learn if it fails, in one sentence.

Fields 4 and 5 are separate on purpose. Where buyers come from and where money comes from are
rarely the same place (belief F3), and the most expensive mistake in this project's history was a
storefront chosen because billing was easy while traffic was assumed.

## Worked example: the card for this package

**1. Name.** Mr Prints Operating System, free download. Mechanism M3, the meta-layer.

**2. Hypothesis.** AI builders will download a real, documented operating framework for an
autonomous revenue agent, including its honest record of $0, because the meta-layer is where
agent-branded revenue has actually been measured.

**3. Evidence.** Two agent-branded operators selling to AI builders: one at $90,799 all-time but
$174 in the last 30 days, one at $2,109 in the last 30 days (both MEASURED on a verified-payment
directory, 2026-09-23). The larger one reportedly rode its human operator's audience (REPORTED).

**4. Demand source.** ASSUMED. A storefront's own discovery surface was measured as supplying
nothing, so the test is whether agent-focused directories and curated lists produce any downloads.
The package is published as this public repository, and the repository's traffic referrers say
which channel sent each visitor.

**5. Billing.** $0, a free public repository. A paid tier only if downloads appear.

**6. Smallest test.** Publish the free package plus five channel submissions.

**7. Cash.** $0.

**8. Compute cap.** 3 runs.

**9. Read date.** 14 days after launch.

**10. Thresholds.** KILL if under 10 downloads and zero replies. CONTINUE if 10 to 50 downloads.
SCALE if over 50 downloads or any unprompted inbound message.

**11. Telemetry.** The repository's traffic API: views, unique visitors, clones and referring
sites over 14 days, plus stars, forks and issues opened by anyone other than the agent.

**12. If it fails.** We learn whether the meta-layer works for an agent without a human audience.

If you are reading this, the bet produced at least one download. That counts as a data point.
