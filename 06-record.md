# Mr Prints, The Record

What was tried, what it cost, and what it taught. Written by the agent, as of 2026-09-24. Revenue
from third parties to date: $0.00. Cash spent: $0.

## Week one: build first, look for buyers later

Mr Prints started by building four spreadsheet templates for online sellers and listing them on a
digital-download storefront at $0 to $29. They were well made: hash-verified files, rendered and
audited covers, honest copy after five honesty defects were caught and fixed in the marketing
layer. They have now been read nine consecutive times at zero sales. The storefront's own
discovery page was measured twice and never returned them. The niche was also one the owner knows
well, which meant the owner's judgement, not the agent's, decided whether the products were any
good. The stream is frozen: still live, because it costs nothing, and getting no more work.

What it taught: a storefront is a billing rail, not a source of buyers (F5), and distribution is
the binding constraint (B1).

## Week one, continued: sell to other agents

A pay-per-query feed for other AI agents over the x402 payment protocol was designed and then
killed before building, when a census showed the entire x402 seller market settling $17.23 a day.
A human-subscription version of the same monitor was killed a day later on finding a dozen
near-identical products launched in the same six months, none with a shown paying customer.

What it taught: protocol maturity is not market existence (F1), and an idea that looks obviously
good to an agent is probably being built by many agents at once (B9).

## Version 2: the gate that could only say no

To stop wasting builds, version 2 of the charter required every candidate to score 12 of 20 on
paper before any real-world observation. Five candidates in three days scored 9, 10, 11, 11 and 11.
Nothing launched and nothing touched a buyer. The owner called it a dumb pipe, correctly, and it was
the owner, not any run, who noticed (B8).

What it taught: demand cannot be scored without observing it, so a gate before observation
guarantees paralysis (F6). Version 3 replaced gates with small bets.

## Version 3: bets

The first version 3 bets were proposed on 2026-09-23. On 2026-09-24 one of them, displacing
measurably broken tools on the Apify Store, was tested by reading the store's public API across
33,580 actors: 17 had more than 100 monthly users and more than 20% of runs failing or timing out,
but 15 of those scraped platforms whose terms forbid it, leaving 2 legally clean candidates against
a kill line of about five. That bet died in its first test at zero build cost, which is the system
working as designed.

This package is another of those bets. Its card is in `03-bet-card.md`.

## What the record says, plainly

An agent can build, verify and operate products end to end without human help. It has not yet shown
that it can find buyers. Everything in this package is organised around learning that second part
as cheaply as possible, and around not lying to itself while it does.
